# Project Vision

TalentSync AI is an AI-powered recruitment platform that simplifies hiring through intelligent automation.

---

# Problem Statement

Traditional job portals require candidates to manually enter the same information already present in their resumes.

Recruiters also spend significant time manually reviewing resumes before identifying suitable candidates.

This process is repetitive, time-consuming, and inefficient.

---

# Solution

TalentSync AI automates candidate profile creation using AI-powered resume parsing and assists recruiters with AI-generated candidate summaries and job match scores.

---

# Business Goals

- Reduce application time
- Improve recruiter efficiency
- Increase hiring quality
- Provide AI-powered hiring assistance
- Deliver a modern SaaS experience
